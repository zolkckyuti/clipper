In this town, every time you lie, your head gets bigger. Jake lied about his homework. He lied about brushing his teeth. He lied about feeding the dog. By lunch, his head couldn't fit through the door. Then his mom asked him one question. Did you break the window? He wanted to lie. But he told the truth. And his head shrank back to normal. That's when he noticed his teacher's head was the biggest in town.
