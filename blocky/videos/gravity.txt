What if gravity turned off for ten seconds every day at noon? In this town, every bench has a seatbelt. Every car is chained to the road. But today, Leo forgot what time it was. Three. Two. One. Leo floated past the school. Past the trees. Past the clouds. Ten seconds later, gravity came back, and Leo landed right on the roof of the bakery. Now, every day at noon, he delivers the bread.
